Privacy Policy · 隐私政策

Last updated: August 2026 · Truth Cards (cards.enrongpan.com)

What we collect · 我们收集什么

Truth Cards does not require an account and does not collect personal information. Game state (used cards, custom questions, settings, an optional display name you enter) is stored locally on your device.

真心话卡牌无需注册账号，不收集个人信息。游戏进度（已用卡牌、自定义问题、设置、你自行填写的昵称）只保存在你自己的设备上。

Live rooms · 联机房间

If you join a live room, your chosen display name and the shared game state (cards drawn, reactions) are relayed through our database (Supabase) so other players in the room can see them. Rooms are automatically deleted within 24 hours. Anonymous, aggregate draw counts are recorded to improve the question bank.

如果你加入联机房间，你填写的昵称和共享的游戏状态（抽到的卡、表情反应）会经由我们的数据库（Supabase）转发给房间内的其他玩家。房间会在24小时内自动删除。我们会记录匿名的抽卡统计，用于改进题库。

Analytics · 统计

The website uses Vercel Analytics, a cookie-free, privacy-friendly page-view counter. It does not identify individual users.

网站使用 Vercel Analytics 统计访问量，不使用 Cookie，也无法识别具体用户。
